## 2024-07-26 - Improve form accessibility and clickable areas
**Learning:** In the FilmSync popup, clickable areas for labels and options were constrained because text descriptions were wrapped in `<span>` or `<div>` rather than proper `<label for="...">` elements linked to their inputs. Additionally, icon/emoji-only avatar buttons lacked `aria-label`s, causing screen readers to rely on OS emoji definitions which may lack context.
**Action:** Always link text descriptions to form inputs using `<label for="[inputId]">` with `cursor: pointer` to maximize the interactive hit area. For decorative emojis inside buttons, hide them with `<span aria-hidden="true">` and provide a clear, localized `aria-label` on the button itself to optimize screen reader output without redundancy.
