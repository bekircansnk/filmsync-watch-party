## 2024-05-18 - Improve accessibility and click targets by linking custom UI text to inputs
**Learning:** In custom UI components (like the host-only switch or section titles acting as headers for form inputs), labels and texts were decoupled from the actual form inputs. This makes it difficult for screen readers to associate the text with the input and results in smaller click targets for users.
**Action:** Always link descriptive texts and headers to their corresponding inputs using `<label for="inputId">` or `aria-labelledby`. For elements styled as text or headers (like `.section-title` or `.switch-text`), converting them to labels and using `cursor: pointer` not only improves screen reader accessibility but significantly increases the click target area for mouse users. For groups of buttons like the avatar list, use `role="group"` and `aria-labelledby`.
