## 2026-08-13 - Enhance Accessibility of Interactive Emojis and Form Elements
**Learning:** In UIs that heavily use emojis or custom spans as interactive buttons (like the avatar selector), visual appearance alone isn't sufficient for screen reader users or keyboard-only users. Similarly, switch inputs and text fields without distinct visible labels need clear programmatic associations to provide context.
**Action:** Always wrap or convert visual switch descriptions into interactive `<label>` elements linked to the input's ID, which automatically expands the click target. For non-standard interactive elements (like the emoji avatar badge), explicitly assign `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, an `aria-label`, and attach a `keydown` listener to handle 'Enter' and 'Space' actions to ensure a fully accessible and intuitive experience.
