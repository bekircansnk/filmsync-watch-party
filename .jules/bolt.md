## 2026-07-19 - Optimize sidebar layout for DOM reflow
**Learning:** Frequent toggling of CSS properties that affect layout (like `width` and `left`) on the `body` element causes expensive DOM reflow operations, especially when resizing complex pages with video players like Netflix, YouTube, or Disney+. This degrades video playback frame rates and increases main-thread blocking time during watch parties. Utilizing `padding-right` with `box-sizing: border-box` on the body reduces the recalculation scope without breaking the video player's internal UI controls, ensuring high-framerate playback.
**Action:** Replaced computationally expensive `width` and `left` property recalculations in the `body.filmsync-sidebar-open` with `padding-right: 270px` and `box-sizing: border-box` to minimize DOM reflow overhead and guarantee high-framerate playback, while preserving the original sizing strategy for the internal video player elements to prevent UI breakage.
