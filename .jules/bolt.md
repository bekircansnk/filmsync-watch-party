## 2026-08-01 - Throttle untamed continuous global events
**Learning:** Untamed continuous global events (like `mousemove` or `scroll`) interacting with DOM queries during full-screen video playback can cause severe DOM recalculation storms and main thread contention, leading to dropped frames and high CPU usage. The specific issue observed was a `mousemove` listener on the document firing constantly over a full-screen video without any throttling.
**Action:** Always throttle or debounce frequent global events (like `mousemove`, `resize`, `scroll`). A simple timestamp-based check (e.g., executing at most once every 250ms) is effective. Additionally, use `{ passive: true }` when adding global listeners that don't need to call `preventDefault()`, allowing the browser to optimize scrolling and input handling without waiting on the main thread.
